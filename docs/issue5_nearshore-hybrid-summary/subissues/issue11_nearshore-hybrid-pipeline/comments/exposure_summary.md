# Issue #11: red tide exposure per monthly map, VIIRS-only vs nearshore hybrid

Produced by `docs/issue11_nearshore-hybrid-pipeline/check_hybrid_issue11.R` (stage `exposure`) from the combined stacks of the `none` run (the 6 October 2026 maps) and the `mask5` run, all 504 months 1985-2026. **Total exposure** of a monthly map is the plain sum of cells/L over the 3838 template water cells (Ecospace works per cell, so no area weighting; the water-cell count is the same in every map). **Cell counts** are the grid cells at or above 10,000, 100,000 and 1,000,000 cells/L. **Samples included** are the in-situ water-cell samples at or above 10,000 cells/L (and the high class) whose 5-arcmin cell is non-zero in the map, the converse of the samples the footprint cuts. Yearly and group totals are sums over the months (cell-months). The two methods are identical outside the 155 VIIRS months (checked).

## Overall

- Over the 155 VIIRS months the hybrid raises total exposure by 10.2% (4,119,726,916 to 4,538,271,356 cells/L summed) and the share of the in-situ water samples >= 10,000 cells/L that the projection includes from 53.2% to 95.1% (4,655 to 8,316 of 8,745; +41.9 percentage points, x 1.79); for the high samples (> 1,000,000 cells/L) from 63.7% to 98.5% (of 1,168).
- Over all 504 months 1985-2026 the hybrid raises total exposure by 4.7% (8,874,996,166 to 9,293,540,606 cells/L summed) and the share of the in-situ water samples >= 10,000 cells/L that the projection includes from 72.4% to 93.0% (12,861 to 16,522 of 17,760; +20.6 percentage points, x 1.28); for the high samples (> 1,000,000 cells/L) from 78.5% to 96.9% (of 2,195).
