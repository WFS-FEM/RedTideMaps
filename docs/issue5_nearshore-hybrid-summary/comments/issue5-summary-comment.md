## Summary and decision record, for review (22 Oct 2026)

The work of the sub-issues (#6, #8, #11, #10, on the hull method of #3) is summarised in one document for review before the call this issue is waiting on: `docs/issue5_nearshore-hybrid-summary/README.md` (https://github.com/WFS-FEM/RedTideMaps/blob/main/docs/issue5_nearshore-hybrid-summary/README.md). Plain language first (the question, what each step found, the method change, what changes in the maps, the decisions with dates and sources, what is pending), then a technical appendix (the rule as implemented, configuration, the verification chain, the numbers, the evidence files, a timeline). `docs/README.md` now indexes the issue folders.

In short: the samples the satellites miss sit in a 0 to 5 km coastal strip the VIIRS product cannot observe; the nearshore hybrid adds the sample-based hull footprint inside that strip and leaves the satellite result alone everywhere else. It lifts the share of in-situ samples >= 10,000 cells/L the maps include from 53% to 95% in the VIIRS era, 82% to 89% in the MODIS era and 72% to 95% over the record, and raises total exposure by 10% in the VIIRS era and 5% over the record; hull months are untouched, and with the rule off the pipeline reproduces the 6 October maps byte for byte.

**The call** (one decision for both sensors): accept the `mask5` maps, after which both defaults flip, the maps are regenerated once and committed, and the drop goes to Ecospace; or keep the satellite-only maps. The implementation plan follows the call.
