# Issue #8: nearshore samples and a VIIRS + hull hybrid

Diagnostics for [WFS-FEM/RedTideMaps#8](https://github.com/WFS-FEM/RedTideMaps/issues/8), the sub-issue of [#5](https://github.com/WFS-FEM/RedTideMaps/issues/5) (VIIRS months with no positive cell are written as all-zero maps) that asks whether the high-count samples VIIRS does not see are nearshore, and whether a hybrid footprint (VIIRS offshore, buffered hulls nearshore) would recover them. Branch `8-explore-nearshore`, PR #9. The plan, with the decisions it needs, is `issue8-nearshore-plan.md`. Nothing in the pipeline or the deliverables is changed by anything in this folder; every number is read from the stacks the 6 October 2026 run left on disk and from the issue-6 tables.

### Definitions

- **FWC class**: the abundance classes of the FWC in-situ maps (myfwc.com/research/redtide/statewide/), right-closed as in the #6 count boxes: negative (0 cells/L), background (above 0 to 1,000), very low (1,000 to 10,000), low (10,000 to 100,000), medium (100,000 to 1,000,000), high (above 1,000,000). Columns named `n_ge_1e4` use >= 10,000 cells/L as in #6, which differs from the class split only at exactly 10,000 cells/L.
- **VIIRS cell status** of a sample: the value of the 0.1-degree cell the sample falls in, in the VIIRS layer of the sample's month: `masked` (NA in the tif), `valid_zero` (0), `valid_pos` (> 0, that is, the pipeline counts the cell as bloom). Samples of 2022-12 (no tif) are `no_layer`; 55 negative samples just outside the grid box are `off_grid`; both are left out of the class tables.
- **The static mask**: the 0.1-degree cells with no data in at least 90% of the 155 months (1,004 cells; 1,001 of them in every month). 15 cells with no data in a single month, 14 of them over deep water in the south-west corner, are a transient gap and not part of it. **Gulf domain**: template water cells outside the Atlantic-side box that `fn.filter_hab_data()` drops samples from (east of -82 E, north of 28.5 N), which the VIIRS product does not cover.
- **Distance to the coast**: from the sample to the Natural Earth 1:10m coastline, in km (UTM 17N, coastline densified to 200 m points), good to about 1 to 2 km. **Distance to the nearest valid cell**: to the centre of the nearest VIIRS cell that is valid in every month.
- **Region** (for the band table only): Panhandle (west of -84 E), Big Bend (28.6 N and north), Tampa Bay to Charlotte Harbor (26.5 to 28.6 N), Lee County to the Keys (south of 26.5 N).

### Results (6 October 2026 run)

1. **The mask is a nearshore band of 0 to 5 km.** On the 5-arcmin template the static mask covers 84 of the 3,782 Gulf water cells (6,316 km2, 2% of the water): 69% of the water cells within 2 km of the coast, 53% of those at 2 to 5 km, 5% at 5 to 10 km and none beyond. Its cells have a median template depth of 2.2 m (maximum 15 m) and their centres a median distance to the coast of 1.0 km (90th percentile 4.5 km). It also covers the bays (Tampa Bay, Charlotte Harbor, Apalachicola Bay, Choctawhatchee Bay) that the template calls land. That is the 2 to 5 km band Yao described.
2. **The mask takes the same share of every abundance class**, 61 to 66% (T1): of the 97,459 samples 62,416 are masked; 1,076 of the 1,627 high samples, 2,995 of the 4,744 medium ones. Of the samples VIIRS does evaluate, the share in a cell with a detection rises from 30% of the background samples to 63% of the low, 76% of the medium and 88% of the high ones.
3. **In the 58 zeroed months** (T2), 70% of the low, medium and high samples are in masked cells (860 of 1,236) and 30% in valid cells that saw nothing (376); 28% are on template land. The 16 headline months are the same: 70% masked, 30% valid-zero. Four headline months are the exception, with most of their high-count samples in valid cells well offshore (2012-03, 2019-02, 2020-01 at a median 48 km from the coast, 2020-11): there VIIRS looked and saw nothing.
4. **In the 60 VIIRS-positive months of #6** (T3), 79% of the 3,186 water samples >= 10,000 cells/L outside the satellite footprint are in masked cells (2,532), 21% in valid-zero cells (654), none in a valid-positive cell: the 41% of #6 is a mask effect, not a resolution artefact. For the high class 91% of the outside samples are masked. (Masked samples can still fall inside the final footprint, 1,961 do, when their 5-arcmin cell centre lies in a neighbouring positive 0.1-degree cell.)
5. **Distance to the coast** (T4): masked samples sit at a median of 0.9 to 1.0 km from the coast whatever their class, 90% within about 3 km. Samples in valid cells with a detection sit at a median of 2.3 (high) to 4.7 km (low, medium). The high samples in valid cells with no detection are also nearshore (median 0.8 km, 60% within 2 km): a 0.1-degree cell that straddles the coast can be valid while the sample in it is in the strip the satellite cannot resolve. Of all high samples 74% are within 2 km of the coast, 87% within 5 km and 93% within 10 km; within 2 km 78% are masked, beyond 10 km none.
6. **What a mask-only rule would restore** (T5): in the 16 headline months the hull-clipped map has 59,637 km2 of bloom area (>= 10,000 cells/L), of which 14,180 km2 (24%) lies in the mask. The remaining three quarters is in VIIRS-valid cells that saw nothing, which is decision D4 of the plan (VIIRS zero stands, or hull). A band of 5 km from the coast would also cover the nearshore valid-zero samples of point 5; both definitions are prototyped in step 2.

Reading for the two hypotheses of the plan: H1 (nearshore mask) accounts for 70 to 79% of the high-count samples VIIRS misses, H2 (valid but not detected) for 21 to 30%, and H3 (resolution) for none. Most of the H2 samples are themselves within 2 km of the coast.
