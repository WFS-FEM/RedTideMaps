# Issue #8: nearshore samples and a VIIRS + hull hybrid

Diagnostics for [WFS-FEM/RedTideMaps#8](https://github.com/WFS-FEM/RedTideMaps/issues/8), the sub-issue of [#5](https://github.com/WFS-FEM/RedTideMaps/issues/5) (VIIRS months with no positive cell are written as all-zero maps) that asks whether the high-count samples VIIRS does not see are nearshore, and whether a hybrid footprint (VIIRS offshore, buffered hulls nearshore) would recover them. Branch `8-explore-nearshore`, PR #9. The plan, with the decisions it needs, is `issue8-nearshore-plan.md`. Nothing in the pipeline or the deliverables is changed by anything in this folder; every number is read from the stacks the 6 October 2026 run left on disk and from the issue-6 tables.

### Definitions

- **FWC class**: the abundance classes of the FWC in-situ maps (myfwc.com/research/redtide/statewide/), right-closed as in the #6 count boxes: negative (0 cells/L), background (above 0 to 1,000), very low (1,000 to 10,000), low (10,000 to 100,000), medium (100,000 to 1,000,000), high (above 1,000,000). Columns named `n_ge_1e4` use >= 10,000 cells/L as in #6, which differs from the class split only at exactly 10,000 cells/L.
- **VIIRS cell status** of a sample: the value of the 0.1-degree cell the sample falls in, in the VIIRS layer of the sample's month: `masked` (NA in the tif), `valid_zero` (0), `valid_pos` (> 0, that is, the pipeline counts the cell as bloom). Samples of 2022-12 (no tif) are `no_layer`; 55 negative samples just outside the grid box are `off_grid`; both are left out of the class tables.
- **The static mask**: the 0.1-degree cells with no data in at least 90% of the 155 months (1,004 cells; 1,001 of them in every month). 15 cells with no data in a single month, 14 of them over deep water in the south-west corner, are a transient gap and not part of it. **Gulf domain**: template water cells outside the Atlantic-side box that `fn.filter_hab_data()` drops samples from (east of -82 E, north of 28.5 N), which the VIIRS product does not cover.
- **Distance to the coast**: from the sample to the Natural Earth 1:10m coastline, in km (UTM 17N, coastline densified to 200 m points), good to about 1 to 2 km. **Distance to the nearest valid cell**: to the centre of the nearest VIIRS cell that is valid in every month.
- **Region** (for the band table only): Panhandle (west of -84 E), Big Bend (28.6 N and north), Tampa Bay to Charlotte Harbor (26.5 to 28.6 N), Lee County to the Keys (south of 26.5 N).

### Results (6 October 2026 run)

1. **The mask is a nearshore band of 0 to 5 km.** On the 5-arcmin template the static mask covers 84 of the 3,782 Gulf water cells (6,316 km2, 2% of the water): 69% of the water cells within 2 km of the coast, 53% of those at 2 to 5 km, 5% at 5 to 10 km and none beyond. Its cells have a median template depth of 2.2 m (maximum 15 m) and their centres a median distance to the coast of 1.0 km (90th percentile 4.5 km). It also covers the bays (Tampa Bay, Charlotte Harbor, Apalachicola Bay, Choctawhatchee Bay) that the template calls land. That is the 2 to 5 km band Yao described.
2. **The mask takes the same share of every abundance class**, 61 to 66% (T1): of the 97,459 samples 62,416 are masked; 1,076 of the 1,627 high samples, 2,995 of the 4,744 medium ones. Of the samples VIIRS does evaluate, the share in a cell with a detection rises from 30% of the background samples to 63% of the low, 76% of the medium and 88% of the high ones.
3. **In the 58 zeroed months** (T2), 70% of the low, medium and high samples are in masked cells (860 of 1,236) and 30% in valid cells that saw nothing (376); 28% are on template land. The 16 headline months are the same: 70% masked, 30% valid-zero. Four headline months are the exception, with most of their high-count samples in valid cells well offshore (2012-03, 2019-02, 2020-01 at a median 48 km from the coast, 2020-11): there VIIRS looked and saw nothing.
4. **In the 60 VIIRS-positive months of #6** (T3), 79% of the 3,186 water samples >= 10,000 cells/L outside the satellite footprint are in masked cells (2,532), 21% in valid-zero cells (654), none in a valid-positive cell: the 41% of #6 is a mask effect, not a resolution artefact. For the high class 91% of the outside samples are masked. (Masked samples can still fall inside the final footprint, 1,961 do, when their 5-arcmin cell centre lies in a neighbouring positive 0.1-degree cell.)
5. **Distance to the coast** (T4): masked samples sit at a median of 0.9 to 1.0 km from the coast whatever their class, 90% within about 3 km. Samples in valid cells with a detection sit at a median of 2.3 (high) to 4.7 km (low, medium). The high samples in valid cells with no detection are also nearshore (median 0.8 km, 60% within 2 km): a 0.1-degree cell that straddles the coast can be valid while the sample in it is in the strip the satellite cannot resolve. Of all high samples 74% are within 2 km of the coast, 87% within 5 km and 93% within 10 km; within 2 km 78% are masked, beyond 10 km none.
6. **What a mask-only rule would restore** (T5): in the 16 headline months the hull-clipped map has 59,637 km2 of bloom area (>= 10,000 cells/L), of which 14,180 km2 (24%) lies in the mask. The remaining three quarters is in VIIRS-valid cells that saw nothing, which is decision D4 of the plan (VIIRS zero stands, or hull). A band of 5 km from the coast would also cover the nearshore valid-zero samples of point 5; both definitions are prototyped in step 2.

Reading for the two hypotheses of the plan: H1 (nearshore mask) accounts for 70 to 79% of the high-count samples VIIRS misses, H2 (valid but not detected) for 21 to 30%, and H3 (resolution) for none. Most of the H2 samples are themselves within 2 km of the coast.

## Step 2: hybrid footprints for four nearshore sets

```
Rscript docs/issue8_nearshore-hybrid/hybrid_nearshore_issue8.R   # ~40 s; sources step 1 first
#   RT_QA_MONTHLY_PDF=0   skip the monthly PDF (~20 s)
```

For every one of the 155 VIIRS months and each nearshore set N, the hybrid footprint is

    hybrid_N[cell] = max(viirs_clipped[cell], hull_clipped[cell] * [cell in N])

Outside N the VIIRS result stands, zeros included (Yao's condition); inside N the buffered-hull footprint is added and never removes a VIIRS detection (the union rule). The hull is the pipeline's own hull-clipped stack, built as for the months without satellite data (every positive sample, 75 km linkage, 10 km buffer); nothing is refit or re-clipped. Comparators: `current` (the final map) and `hull` (the hull-clipped map, what a full hull fallback would give).

### Definitions

- **Sets** (5-arcmin Gulf-domain water cells): `mask` = cells whose 0.1-degree VIIRS cell is in the static no-data mask (84 cells, 6,316 km2); `coast2`, `coast5`, `coast10` = cells whose centre is within 2, 5, 10 km of the Natural Earth 1:10m coastline (64 / 132 / 213 cells; 4,812 / 9,922 / 16,015 km2). The coast sets are nested; `coast5` holds 80 of the 84 mask cells and `coast10` all of them; `coast2` only 44.
- **Metrics per month and variant**: footprint and bloom area in km2 of water cells; `added` = area the variant adds to the current map, `added_*_masked` / `added_*_valid` by the VIIRS status of the cell's 0.1-degree cell that month; `removed` = current footprint the variant drops (0 for every hybrid by construction; non-zero only for `hull`); `in_low / in_medium / in_high / in_ge1e4` = water samples of the month whose 5-arcmin cell is non-zero in the variant. Land samples are not counted (step 1 reports them).
- **Control months**: VIIRS-positive fitted months of #6 with no flag, at least 80% of the water samples >= 10,000 cells/L inside the current footprint and a hull ratio within 0.75 to 1.33; the two with the highest share (2014-08, 2021-09), asserted by the script.

### Results (6 October 2026 run)

| variant | samples >= 10,000 cells/L inside, all 155 months | high samples inside, all months | bloom area added, all months (km2 >= 10,000 cells/L) | of which in VIIRS-valid cells | hull bloom area restored, 58 zeroed months | high samples inside, 58 zeroed months |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| current | 53% | 64% | 0 | 0 | 0% | 0% |
| mask | 88% | 96% | 90,389 | 227 | 24% | 63% |
| coast2 | 80% | 91% | 65,182 | 13,026 | 17% | 34% |
| coast5 | 91% | 97% | 118,206 | 32,143 | 35% | 81% |
| coast10 | 98% | 99% | 161,811 | 71,649 | 54% | 97% |
| hull | 100% | 100% | 358,244 | 268,083 | 100% | 100% |

1. **Every hybrid lifts sample coverage a lot and changes the maps only near the coast.** The share of water samples >= 10,000 cells/L inside the footprint goes from 53% (current) to 88% (`mask`), 91% (`coast5`) and 98% (`coast10`); for the high class from 64% to 96 to 99%. In the 60 VIIRS-positive fitted months of #6 the hybrids add 7 to 17% to a current bloom area of 776,173 km2; in the two control months they add nothing (2014-08) or 5 to 9% (2021-09).
2. **`mask` adds only where VIIRS has no data** (227 of 90,389 km2 in valid cells, from the three cells masked in 145 to 152 months). It restores 24% of the hull-clipped bloom area in the 58 zeroed months and covers 59% of their samples >= 10,000 cells/L (63% of the high ones); the rest of those samples sit in valid cells that saw nothing.
3. **`coast2` is below `mask` on every measure** (smaller inside the bays, 40 mask cells outside it) while overriding 13,026 km2 of valid cells. **`coast5`** nearly contains the mask (80 of 84 cells) and adds a 2 to 5 km strip of valid cells: 32,143 km2 of valid-cell area over 155 months (7,571 km2 in the zeroed months), for 81% of the zeroed months' high samples. **`coast10`** reaches 97 to 99% of the high samples but 71,649 km2 of its additions are in valid cells, more than its masked additions.
4. **The hull alone is not a substitute for VIIRS:** used instead of the satellite it would drop 438,288 km2 of the VIIRS footprint over the 66 VIIRS-positive months (the union rule avoids that), which is why `hull` is a reference column, not a candidate.
5. **Headline months** (`fig6`): 2017-01 goes from 0 to 1,821 (`mask`), 2,276 (`coast5`), 3,341 (`coast10`) km2 of bloom area against 4,181 for the hull, with 74 / 100 / 113 of its 116 samples >= 10,000 cells/L inside; 2021-06 from 0 to 2,044 / 2,731 / 4,027 km2 (hull 7,361) with 144 / 132 / 183 of 188 samples. The four months whose high-count samples sit offshore in valid cells (2012-03, 2019-02, 2020-01, 2020-11) stay almost unchanged under every set.

Reading for decision D1: `mask` is the parameter-free option that respects Yao's condition exactly; `coast5` is the option that also reaches the nearshore valid-zero samples of step 1, at the cost of overriding a 2 to 5 km strip where VIIRS reported no bloom; `coast2` and `coast10` bracket them. Decision D4 (what the VIIRS-valid area gets in all-zero months) is visible in the `hull` column of the zeroed-month totals.

### Checks

The script asserts that every hybrid equals the current map outside its set and the cell-wise maximum of the current and hull-clipped maps inside it, never below the current map and never above that maximum; that an empty set reproduces the current map and the whole domain the full union; that the sets are nested and the areas monotone; that per-month water-sample counts by class equal the step-1 month table and the counts of samples >= 10,000 cells/L on water and inside the current footprint equal the #6 month table; and that the control-month rule reproduces the frozen months. CSVs, PNGs and the summary are byte-identical across reruns; the two PDFs (fig6 and the monthly file) differ only in the two date lines every R `pdf()` device writes. (The issue plan's single `fig5_hybrid_headline.png` became fig5 to fig7; fig6 is a PDF because a 20-row PNG was too coarse to read.)
