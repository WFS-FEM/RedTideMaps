# Issue #11: red tide exposure per monthly map, VIIRS-only vs nearshore hybrid

Produced by `docs/issue11_nearshore-hybrid-pipeline/check_hybrid_issue11.R` (stage `exposure`) from the combined stacks of the `none` run (the 6 October 2026 maps) and the `mask5` run, all 504 months 1985-2026. **Total exposure** of a monthly map is the plain sum of cells/L over the 3838 template water cells (Ecospace works per cell, so no area weighting; the water-cell count is the same in every map). **Cell counts** are the grid cells at or above 10,000, 100,000 and 1,000,000 cells/L.
