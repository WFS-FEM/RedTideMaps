# FWC red tide evidence — 17 requested months

Selected dated reports for Southwest Florida and the adjacent West Florida Shelf; not whole-month summaries.

Quotation marks identify verbatim excerpts; … marks omissions. Unquoted text is summarized. Source details and limitations are in the Sources and notes table below.

**Coverage:** Narrative reports or credited reproductions were recovered for 16 months. April 2013 remains unverified. “Not stated” is distinct from an explicit report of no impacts.

| All months | Coverage | Method | One accessible dated narrative report was selected for each month where one could be recovered. April 2017 includes a second dated observation to show within-month change. Selection was based on recoverability and usable detail, not a systematic monthly maximum, monthly mean, or fixed day of month. |
| All months | Quotation convention | Method | Quoted passages preserve the retrieved wording, with typographic whitespace normalized. Ellipses mark omitted words. These are short verbatim excerpts, not full paragraphs. Summaries are outside quotation marks. The April 2017 supplemental fish quotation is explicitly attributed to SFWMD. |
| All months | Missing-data convention | Method | Not stated means the retrieved bulletin or reproduction omits the topic. Not verified means the required narrative was not retrieved. Neither means no events occurred. An explicit negative statement applies only to the report’s specified sampling or reporting interval. |
| All months | Geographic scope | Method | The table follows FWC’s Southwest Florida reporting geography, including Pinellas/Hillsborough south toward Monroe where reported. Coastal, estuarine and offshore locations are distinguished when the source does so. The evidence does not establish uniform conditions over the entire West Florida Shelf. |
| All months | Concentration interpretation | [FWC concentration key](https://myfwc.com/research/redtide/statewide/) | Categories in the main table are reported ranges, not measured maxima for the whole month. Counts in parentheses are numbers of samples associated with those ranges, not numbers of bloom-level samples unless explicitly stated.<br>Background: ≤1,000 cells/L; very low: &gt;1,000–10,000; low: &gt;10,000–100,000; medium: &gt;100,000–1,000,000; high: &gt;1,000,000. |
| All months | Bloom terminology | Method | FWC’s historical narrative wording is retained. Some older reports call the event a bloom even when that week’s listed concentrations peak at low. The June 2021 and May 2023 reports explicitly use &gt;100,000 cells/L as their bloom-concentration criterion. Do not infer a bloom merely from a background detection. |
| All months | Impact interpretation | Method | Fish kills and respiratory irritation are reports received or observations described by the source. Their presence alongside K. brevis samples does not alone prove causation. The source’s explicit suspected-to-be-related qualification is preserved. A count of received reports is not a count of dead fish. |
| All months | Research date | 2026-10-15 | Sources checked October 15, 2026. Sixteen months have a retrieved narrative or credited reproduction; April 2013 remains a narrative-evidence gap. No concentration values were inferred from an inaccessible map image. |
